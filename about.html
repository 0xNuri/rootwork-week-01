About Us

Welcome to Magic Makers International

between the last coat in the wardrobe and the first snowfall of an enchanted forest, there is a place where silver glows like moonlight and gemstones hold a little bit of wonder. That is where our jewellery begins.
Emberwood & Frost was born from a simple belief: the most beautiful things feel like a secret you've been waiting to find. Every piece we make is inspired by hidden doorways, lantern-lit woods, winter kingdoms, and the quiet magic of stories you never quite outgrow.

Our Story

We started as a small workshop with a single bench, a handful of tools, and a head full of fairy tales.
Each design begins as a sketch of something half-remembered from a dream: a crown of frosted branches,
a pendant shaped like a faraway star, a ring that looks like it was forged at midnight.
Today, our artisans still shape every piece by hand, using ethically sourced stones and recycled metals, because enchantment should never come at the world's expense.

What We Create
  • Winterlight Collection: icy moonstones, crystal drops, and silver that shimmers like fresh frost
  • Wildwood Collection: leaves, vines, and acorns cast in warm gold and bronze
  • Starborne Collection: celestial pendants and constellation rings for dreamers and wanderers
  • Crown & Court: tiaras, circlets, and statement pieces fit for royalty

Our Promise

Every piece arrives in a keepsake box tied with ribbon, ready to be given, or kept as a secret of your own. We believe jewellery should do more than decorate. It should make you feel like the hero of your own story.
